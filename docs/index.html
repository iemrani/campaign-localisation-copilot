Campaign Localisation Copilot

An agentic AI workflow that takes a single marketing brief and produces localised campaign assets across markets and channels, with human approvals, automated compliance checks and a full audit trail.

One run ingests a brief, extracts a structured spec with a language model, then generates nine localised drafts across three markets and three channels, flagging every claim that violates the brief's own rules.
